Ingredients Watchlist

🚫 1. The "Hateful Eight" Seed Oils

Industrial polyunsaturated seed oils extracted via chemical solvents and heat, highly prone to lipid oxidation.

Canola Oil (Rapeseed)

Heavily deodorized industrial seed oil.

Soybean Oil

Dominant restaurant & salad dressing oil, high linoleic acid.

Corn Oil

High omega-6 inflammatory ratio.

Cottonseed Oil

Non-food crop byproduct oil often treated with pesticides.

Sunflower & Safflower Oil

High-omega-6 PUFA oils unless explicitly certified 100% high-oleic.

Grapeseed & Rice Bran Oil

Industrial oils up to 70% linoleic acid.

Hydrogenated Vegetable Oil / Margarine

Damaging trans-fat precursors.

🌽 2. Starch Fillers & Bulking Agents

Refined industrial carbohydrates used to add cheap bulk and alter texture.

Maltodextrin

Glycemic Index of 110–185 (higher than pure table sugar; spikes insulin rapidly).

Modified Food / Corn Starch

Enzymatically altered cheap filler thickener.

Dextrin & Tapioca Dextrin

Processed starch binding agents.

Soluble Corn Fiber / Polydextrose

Synthetic isolated fibers used to artificially manipulate net carb counts.

Carrageenan

Seaweed extract thickener linked to gut microbiome disruption.

Xanthan Gum, Guar Gum & Cellulose Gum

Industrial binders; may cause digestive bloating or GI distress in sensitive guts.

🧪 3. Preservatives & Synthetic Additives

Synthetic chemical stabilizers used to extend shelf-life for months or years.

BHA, BHT & TBHQ

Petroleum-derived synthetic antioxidants to prevent fat rancidity.

Sodium Benzoate & Potassium Sorbate

Chemical antimicrobials (benzoate can form benzene when paired with Vitamin C).

Sodium Nitrite & Nitrate

Curing salts that can form carcinogenic nitrosamines when fried or cooked at high heat.

Disodium EDTA & Calcium Propionate

Heavy metal chelators and bread antifungals.

Artificial Dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6, Blue 1)

Petroleum-based azo dyes linked to behavioral hyperactivity in children.

✨ 4. Clean Standards (What to Choose)
Extra Virgin Olive Oil & Avocado Oil

Cold-pressed, unrefined monounsaturated fats.

Coconut Oil, Grass-Fed Butter, Ghee & Tallow

Heat-stable, traditional ancestral cooking fats.

Single Whole Foods

1 to 5 recognizable ingredients (e.g. 100% beef, organic dates, sea salt, rosemary extract).
